About us

Florence MAPLab

MAPLab is the Multisensory, Action & Perception Laboratory at the University of Florence.

Mission

The laboratory investigates how perception, action and multisensory processing are connected in the brain. Its work spans human research and animal models, with a focus on the sensory, neural and cognitive mechanisms that allow organisms to interpret the world and act adaptively.

MAPLab aims to advance scientific knowledge while supporting innovation, collaboration and the training of new researchers in perception, action and multisensory neuroscience.

Approach

The lab integrates experimental psychology, psychophysics, neurophysiology, eye-tracking, pupillometry and computational modelling. Publicly listed methods include EEG, fMRI, fNIRS, two-photon imaging and patch-clamp electrophysiology.

Research themes

  • Multisensory integration and perceptual dynamics
  • Numerical cognition and links between action and perception
  • Developmental and educational aspects of perception
  • Vision, visual-system development and critical-period plasticity
  • Neuro-aesthetics and the perceptual basis of aesthetic experience
  • Artificial intelligence and machine-learning applications in neuroscience
  • Optical and electrophysiological methods for studying neural activity in animal models

Department

Department of Neuroscience, Psychology, Pharmacology and Child Health — NEUROFARBA.
