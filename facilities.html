Facilities

Research spaces and joint laboratories

MAPLab operates across in-house facilities and joint laboratories equipped for human and animal-model research.

In-house laboratories

Facilities directly listed by MAPLab for psychophysics, eye-tracking, pupillometry and EEG work.

SS

San Salvi Laboratories

Address: Via di S. Salvi, 12/Palazzina 26, 50135 Firenze FI.

Main site for psychophysics, eye-tracking and pupillometry experiments.

FI

Florentine Incubator Laboratory

Address: Via Madonna del Piano, 6, 50019 Sesto Fiorentino FI.

Human-research setup including EEG, dual Purkinje eye-tracking and psychophysics.

Joint laboratories

Collaborative research sites supporting neurophysiology, psychophysics and computational work.

CNR

CNR Laboratory

Address: Via Giuseppe Moruzzi, 56124 Pisa PI.

Joint work with CNR on psychophysical and neurophysiology studies in mice and humans.

LENS

LENS Laboratory

Address: Via Nello Carrara, 1, 50019 Sesto Fiorentino FI.

Joint work with LENS on computational and neurophysiology studies in zebrafish.
