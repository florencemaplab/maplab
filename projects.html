WhEye — Active Temporal Encoding of Spatial Information in Dyslexia

WhEye examines whether spatial representation in vision is closely linked to visuomotor and spatiotemporal coding mechanisms. The project uses reading as a test case: eye movements transform a static written page into a dynamic visual stream that must remain coordinated with visual processing.

The public project page reports that the grant was funded by the University of Florence, with Alessandro Benedetto as coordinator and Giulia Vettori as operating unit manager. The proposal connects active vision, dyslexia, visuomotor synchronization and neural oscillations.
