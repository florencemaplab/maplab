Alessandro Benedetto

Researcher in cognitive neuroscience and psychobiology at the University of Florence, working on human perceptual systems, sensorimotor integration, visual neuroscience and neural dynamics.

AB
  • Role: Fixed-term researcher, Italian Law 240/10
  • SSD: PSIC-01/B – Neuropsychology and cognitive neuroscience
  • Department: NEUROFARBA, University of Florence
  • Email: alessandro.benedetto@unifi.it

  • External profile: PisaVisionLab

Research interests

His research focuses on human perceptual systems and sensorimotor integration, combining psychophysics, EEG, fMRI, eye-movement recordings, computational modelling and pupillometry.

  • Action and perception
  • Eye movements
  • EEG
  • fMRI
  • Neural oscillations
  • Serial dependence
  • Predictive signals

  • 2013: Master’s degree in Psychology, Cognitive Neuroscience, Vita-Salute San Raffaele University.

Selected topics

Current themes include visuomotor communication, neural oscillations, perceptual history and serial dependence, predictive modulation of perception, and the coupling between action and sensory processing.
