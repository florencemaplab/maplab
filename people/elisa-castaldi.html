Elisa Castaldi

Associate Professor at the University of Florence, working on numerical cognition, visual perception, developmental dyscalculia, neuroimaging and plasticity of the visual system.

  • Role: Associate Professor
  • SSD: PSIC-01/B – Neuropsychology and cognitive neuroscience
  • Department: NEUROFARBA, University of Florence
  • Email: elisa.castaldi@unifi.it
  • Official profile: UniFI
  • Personal website: elisacastaldi.org
  • External profile: PisaVisionLab

Research interests

Her research combines behavioural methods, eye tracking, fMRI, EEG and neuropsychological testing to study visual perception, numerical cognition and brain plasticity in typical and clinical populations.

  • Numerosity perception
  • Numerical cognition
  • Dyscalculia
  • Visual perception
  • Plasticity
  • Retinal prosthesis
  • fMRI
  • EEG

CV highlights

  • Current position: Associate Professor, University of Florence.
  • 2021–2022: Fixed-term researcher, NEUROFARBA, University of Florence.
  • 2020–2021: Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowship-linked research appointment, University of Pisa.
  • 2016–2018: Postdoctoral research fellow at INSERM NeuroSpin, France.
  • PhD in Psychology and Neuroscience, University of Florence.

Selected topics

Topics include visual and multisensory perception, dyscalculia, behavioural biomarkers of numerical perception, neurocognitive assessment and visual-system plasticity after visual impairment or restoration.
