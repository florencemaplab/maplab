Faculty profile

Giovanni Anobile

Associate Professor at the University of Florence, studying numerical cognition, numerosity perception, multisensory perception and links between perception, action and mathematical abilities.

  • Role: Associate Professor
  • SSD: PSIC-01/B – Neuropsychology and cognitive neuroscience
  • Department: NEUROFARBA, University of Florence
  • Email: giovanni.anobile@unifi.it
  • ORCID: 0000-0003-2796-0661
  • Official profile: UniFI
  • External profile: PisaVisionLab

Research interests

His main research line concerns numerical cognition, from basic perceptual mechanisms of numerosity to the development of mathematical abilities and dyscalculia, with psychophysical and neuropsychological approaches.

  • Numerosity perception
  • Numerical cognition
  • Multisensory perception
  • Sensorimotor number system
  • Dyscalculia
  • Attention
  • Development

  • 2009: Master’s degree in Experimental Psychology, University of Florence.
  • Research experience across developmental neuropsychology, psychophysics, EEG and fMRI.

Selected topics

Topics include the visual and sensorimotor number systems, numerosity as a precursor of mathematical abilities, typical and atypical development, dyscalculia, attention and multisensory integration.
