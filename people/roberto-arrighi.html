Roberto Arrighi

Professor of neuropsychology and cognitive neuroscience at the University of Florence, studying visual and auditory perception, temporal processing, biological motion and multisensory integration.

  • Role: Full Professor
  • SSD: PSIC-01/B – Neuropsychology and cognitive neuroscience
  • Department: NEUROFARBA, University of Florence
  • Email: roberto.arrighi@unifi.it
  • Official profile: UniFI
  • External profile: PisaVisionLab

Research interests

His work investigates perceptual mechanisms in humans, with emphasis on the visual and auditory systems, spatial and temporal features of sensory stimuli, and biologically plausible models of perception.

  • Multisensory perception
  • Biological motion
  • Temporal perception
  • Visual perception
  • Auditory perception
  • Action–perception interactions
  • Clinical neuropsychology

CV highlights

  • Current position: Full Professor, University of Florence.
  • 2016–2019: Tenure-track researcher, NEUROFARBA, University of Florence.
  • 2012–2016: Fixed-term researcher, NEUROFARBA, University of Florence.
  • 2009–2012: Research fellow, University of Florence.
  • PhD in Psychology and Cognitive Science, University of Florence.

Projects and methods

Research methods include psychophysics, physiological methods, computational approaches and functional neuroimaging. Recent lines include pupillometry, number and time perception, and multimodal spatial encoding.
