Florence MAPLab

The Multisensory, Action & Perception Laboratory studies how perception, action, and multisensory information are linked in the brain.

MAPLab brings together experimental psychology, psychophysics, neurophysiology, eye-tracking, pupillometry and computational modelling to investigate human and animal-model mechanisms of perception and adaptive behaviour.

Research focus

From sensory signals to adaptive behaviour

The laboratory investigates how the brain encodes information, combines signals from multiple sources, guides decisions and supports perception-action loops.

01

Multisensory perception

How visual, sensory-motor and other signals interact to form coherent representations of the world.

02

Vision and action

Eye movements, visual dynamics, perceptual efficiency and the links between perception and motor behaviour.

03

Neural mechanisms

Neurophysiology, computational approaches and advanced optical/electrophysiological methods in human and animal research.

Open lab culture

Interdisciplinary research, training and collaboration

MAPLab combines complementary expertise across perception, action, neuroscience, computational modelling, developmental studies and artificial intelligence applications in neuroscience.
